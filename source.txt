Fraud Prevention and Consequences
Authenticity of Data
	All data regarding likes, views, and audience demographics must be authentic and organically obtained. Any fraudulent or paid views will not be counted.
	Verification
	WebComics reserves the right to audit and verify the authenticity of all performance metrics through third-party tools and analytics.
	Consequences of Fraud
	Forfeiture of Payments: If there is suspicion that the screen recording of the video analysis provided by the creator has been falsified or modified, and if the disparities between the data provided and those tracked by Webcomics are significant, Webcomics reserves the right to withhold, cancel or settle the payment based on the actual circumstances at its discretion.
Termination of Contract: WebComics reserves the right to immediately terminate the contract if fraudulent activities are discovered.
Legal Action: WebComics reserves the right to pursue legal action if fraudulent activities are discovered, including but not limited to seeking damages and legal fees.
Reimbursement: The creator may be required to reimburse any payments received based on fraudulent data.
